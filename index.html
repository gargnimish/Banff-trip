Local Bus System

Alternative to Parks Canada shuttles for reaching Lake Louise and Moraine Lake directly from Banff Town.

  • Route 8X (Banff to Lake Louise Express)

    Reservable online starting May. Highly recommended during peak summer months.

  • Route 10 (Moraine Lake Express)

    Runs seasonally from Banff directly to Moraine Lake. Direct reservations required.

Day 1: Banff Townsite & Surrounding Lakes

Driving: ~45 mins total
  • 08:00 AM - Morning Hike: Tunnel Mountain or Johnston Canyon

    Start early to beat the crowd. Johnston Canyon features lower/upper falls catwalks.

  • 12:00 PM - Lunch & Explore Banff Avenue

    Stroll downtown Banff, visit local bakeries, shops, and the Banff Park Museum.

  • 03:00 PM - Lake Minnewanka & Two Jack Lake Loop

    15-minute drive from town. Perfect for short lakeside walks or an afternoon boat ride.

  • 06:00 PM - Banff Gondola Sunset & Dinner

    Ride to Sulphur Mountain summit for panoramic view over the Bow Valley.

Day 2: Lake Louise & Moraine Lake

Park & Ride Shuttle Required
  • 06:30 AM - Arrive at Park & Ride (Lake Louise Ski Resort)

    Board booked Parks Canada Shuttle to Moraine Lake first for calm morning light on the Ten Peaks.

  • 07:00 AM - Moraine Lake & Rockpile Trail

    Walk up the Rockpile Trail for the iconic turquoise lake perspective.

  • 10:30 AM - Lake Connector Shuttle to Lake Louise

    Use the free connector shuttle between Moraine Lake and Lake Louise (included in shuttle ticket).

  • 11:30 AM - Lake Shore Trail & Tea House Hike

    Hike to Lake Agnes Tea House (3.5 km, moderate) or Plain of Six Glaciers.

Day 3: Icefields Parkway Drive (Banff to Jasper)

Driving: ~230 km (3.5 - 5 hrs with stops)
  • 08:00 AM - Departure & Peyto Lake

    Stop at Bow Lake and hike up to the Bow Summit lookout for Peyto Lake's dog-head view.

  • 11:30 AM - Columbia Icefield Center

    Experience the Ice Explorer Athabasca Glacier Tour and walk the glass Skywalk.

  • 03:30 PM - Waterfalls Route

    Stop at Sunwapta Falls and Athabasca Falls along Hwy 93 into Jasper.

  • 06:00 PM - Arrive in Jasper Townsite

    Check-in to accommodation and enjoy dinner in downtown Jasper.

Day 4: Jasper Lakes & Maligne Valley

Driving: ~1.5 hrs roundtrip
  • 08:30 AM - Maligne Canyon Hike

    Explore deep limestone canyon bridges and cascading waterfalls.

  • 11:00 AM - Medicine Lake & Maligne Lake Cruise

    Drive past Medicine Lake to Maligne Lake for a boat cruise to Spirit Island.

  • 03:30 PM - Pyramid & Patricia Lakes

    Relax, rent a kayak, or stroll across the wooden bridge to Pyramid Island.
